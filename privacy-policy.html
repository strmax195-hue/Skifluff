Skyfluff

Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 19, 2026

In short: Skyfluff does not collect, store on any server, share or sell any personal data. The game works fully offline. It has no ads, no in-app purchases, no accounts and no analytics.

1. Who we are

Skyfluff is a mobile game for Android published by the developer “Max” (“we”, “us”). This policy explains what happens to information when you play the game.

2. Information we collect

We do not collect any personal information. The game does not ask for your name, email, location, contacts, photos or any other personal data, and it does not request any special device permissions.

3. Data stored on your device

To make the game work, a few values are saved only on your own device:

  • your best score and the number of gems you have collected;
  • which tutorial tips you have already seen;
  • your sound setting and chosen language.

This information never leaves your device. You can delete it at any time by clearing the app’s data in your phone settings or by uninstalling the game.

4. Internet, analytics and advertising

The game does not connect to the internet while you play. It contains no advertising, no analytics or tracking tools, no social features and no third-party SDKs that collect data. Crash reporting and analytics services of the game engine are turned off.

5. App stores

When you download the game from Google Play, RuStore or another store, that store may process data about you under its own privacy policy (for example to deliver updates or show install statistics to the developer in aggregated, anonymous form). We have no access to personal data held by the stores.

6. Children

Skyfluff is suitable for players of all ages. Because the game collects no personal information, it does not collect personal information from children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your country).

7. Your rights

Since we hold no personal data about you, there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete. All locally saved progress is under your control as described in section 3. These rights apply under the GDPR, the UK GDPR, the CCPA and the Russian Federal Law No. 152-FZ alike.

8. Changes to this policy

If a future version of the game changes how data is handled (for example by adding online features), we will update this page and the effective date above before that version is released.

9. Contact

Questions about this policy? Write to strmax195@gmail.com or open an issue at github.com/strmax195-hue/Skifluff/issues.
